# 🎥 YouTube Learning Playlists

Your complete self-learning kit. Watch at your own pace — no pressure!

> 💡 **Tip:** Start with #1 or #2. You don't need to watch everything — pick what feels right for you.

---

## 🐣 Start Here (Complete Beginners)

### 1. FreeCodeCamp — Git & GitHub Full Beginner Course
⏱ About 1 hour | Perfect for: Everyone  
A gentle, friendly full course. One video, covers everything from scratch.  
🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RGOj5yH7evk

---

### 2. GitHub's Official Channel
⏱ Short 5–10 min videos | Perfect for: Everyone  
Bite-sized official tutorials from GitHub themselves. Very easy to follow.  
🔗 https://www.youtube.com/@GitHubGuides

---

## 📚 Step-by-Step Series (Go at Your Own Pace)

### 3. The Net Ninja — Git & GitHub Full Series
⏱ 12 short episodes (5–10 min each) | Perfect for: Everyone  
Covers: Why Git? → Creating a repo → Commits → Branches → Collaborating → Contributing  
Great because you can watch ONE episode at a time and stop anytime.  
🔗 https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL4cUxeGkcC9goXbgTDQ0n_4TBzOO0ocPR

---

### 4. Traversy Media — GitHub Crash Course
⏱ Under 30 minutes | Perfect for: Quick refresher  
Fast, practical. Good to re-watch before your next practice session.  
🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SWYqp7iY_Tc

---

## 👩‍💼 For Non-Developers (You Don't Need to Code!)

### 5. GitHub for Non-Developers
⏱ About 30 min | Perfect for: Finance, Teaching, Sales, Logistics, PM  
Specifically made for people who aren't programmers. Uses everyday examples.  
🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BCQHnlnPusY

---

### 6. Programming with Mosh — Git Tutorial for Beginners
⏱ About 1 hour | Perfect for: Visual learners  
Very clear explanations with great visuals. Highly rated by beginners.  
🔗 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8JJ101D3knE

## 🚀 Bonus: GitHub Pages (Build a Free Website!)

### 7. GitHub Pages Tutorial — Build a Free Portfolio Website
⏱ About 20 min | Perfect for: Anyone who wants a personal website
